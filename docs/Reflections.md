## Final consolidation checklist

- Include at least three detailed skill examples for the team.
- Explain how each skill was selected, tested, and corrected.
- Identify both productivity gains and additional work caused by the agent.
- Record changes that would improve the agent's instructions or skill set.
- Ensure every statement matches the verified interaction summaries under `logs/`.

This document is intentionally incomplete until the other team member supplies their own reflection. No reflection is
attributed to a person who has not reviewed it.
